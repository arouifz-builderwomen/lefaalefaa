Oussama — Water Photographer, Bodyboarder & Ocean Storyteller.

Born and raised on the Atlantic coast of Safi, Oussama is a seasoned bodyboarder, surfer and professional water photographer whose life has been shaped by the ocean. Equipped with professional water-housing systems and years of experience in the water, he captures surfing, bodyboarding, swimming, sailing, competitions and aquatic events from perspectives impossible to achieve from shore. RASS LEFAA

The wave that shaped a coastline.

On the western edge of Safi, where ancient cliffs meet the Atlantic Ocean, lies Rass Lefaa. For generations, the sea has shaped this coastline, carving a wave that has become legendary among surfers who know where to look. When the swell arrives from deep in the Atlantic, the reef awakens. Long walls of water draw elegant lines across the bay, creating moments of rare precision and beauty. Surfers travel thousands of miles to experience it. We grew up with it.
